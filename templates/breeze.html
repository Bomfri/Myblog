The Breeze

The Breeze is a new Minecraft mob that was added to the game in the Tricky Trials (1.21) update. This mob spawns in the new trial chambers. It uses wind charges to damage the player and enable traps inside the trial chambers.

Difficulty Easy Normal Hard
Damage * 0.75
Common Questions:
  • How to find the Breeze
    1. To find the Breeze, you first have to locate a trial chamber (trade with the cartographer villager for the map)
    2. Explore through the trial chambers to find a Breeze spawner
  • How to kill the Breeze
    1. The Breeze is not a dangerous mob by itself, try to kill the other mobs in the chamber first
    2. Then you can use a melee weapon to kill the breeze (make sure to have a water bucket to clutch from the fall damage)
